\* Standardized mean difference (laparoscopic completion minus open-related), with values of |SMD| ≥ 0.10 shown in bold. We report SMDs rather than p values because the two groups are not samples from a common population and no null hypothesis of equal baseline distributions is of interest here; the SMD quantifies the size of the imbalance that the analysis must contend with, independently of sample size. Age was taken from the earliest admission record for each child. Necrosis is shown as a marker of index-operation severity, not a baseline demographic.
† Age is strongly right-skewed, so its SMD is computed on the log(days + 1) scale; the median and IQR are shown untransformed, in months.
